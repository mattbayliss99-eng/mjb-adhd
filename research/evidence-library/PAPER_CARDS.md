## mitochondria (7)

### P2017-EMRE · 2017 · [Proteolytic control of the mitochondrial calcium uniporter complex](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5410796/)

**Study level:** primary molecular/cell experiment — cell and biochemical systems.

**What was directly shown:** AFG3L2/SPG7 m-AAA proteases degrade surplus unassembled EMRE; protease-resistant EMRE permits poorly gated calcium entry in experimental systems.

**What it cannot establish:** A direction or magnitude of mitochondrial calcium abnormality in a particular patient or SPG7 genotype.

**Related claims:** C-MCU-ASSEMBLY (direct_model_supported); C-SPG7-DIRECTION (direction_context_dependent).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1073/pnas.1702938114 · PMID: 28396416 · PMCID: PMC5410796. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2019-SPG7-MCU · 2019 · [SPG7 targets the m-AAA protease complex to process MCU for uniporter assembly, Ca2+ influx, and regulation of mitochondrial permeability transition pore opening](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6635443/)

**Study level:** primary cell/mitochondrial perturbation experiments — SPG7-deficient experimental cells.

**What was directly shown:** Loss of SPG7 reduced mitochondrial calcium and altered MCU-complex formation in the studied model; apparent resistance to calcium-induced pore opening tracked lower matrix calcium.

**What it cannot establish:** SPG7 loss always increases matrix calcium or that SPG7 is the pore's structural core.

**Related claims:** C-MCU-ASSEMBLY (direct_model_supported); C-SPG7-DIRECTION (direction_context_dependent).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1074/jbc.RA118.006443 · PMID: 31097542 · PMCID: PMC6635443. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2026-SPG7-NEURONS · 2026 · [Rescue of mitochondrial and neurite pathology in SPG7 hereditary spastic paraplegia patient-derived cortical neurons](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13296996/)

**Study level:** primary translational in-vitro study — cortical neurons derived from 3 SPG7 patients and 3 matched controls.

**What was directly shown:** Abnormal mitochondrial ultrastructure and slower anterograde axonal mitochondrial transport in patient-derived neurons; Bz-423 improved selected in-vitro phenotypes.

**What it cannot establish:** Calcium concentrations directly measured in these neurons, safe clinical efficacy of Bz-423, or an established treatment for HSP.

**Related claims:** C-SPG7-DIRECTION (direction_context_dependent); C-SPG7-AXON (patient_cell_model_supported).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1093/braincomms/fcag219 · PMID: 42367671 · PMCID: PMC13296996. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2025-NCLX-STRUCTURE · 2025 · [Structure and mechanism of the mitochondrial calcium transporter NCLX](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09491-0)

## catecholamines (3)

### P2025-VMAT2-STRUCTURE · 2025 · [Drug inhibition and substrate transport mechanisms of human VMAT2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11695631/)

**Study level:** primary human protein cryo-EM, cell assays and simulation — human VMAT2 structures and heterologous cell transport measurements.

**What was directly shown:** Structures of human VMAT2 bound to dopamine/serotonin and inhibitors reveal substrate-binding states, conformational mechanisms and importance of a vesicular proton gradient.

**What it cannot establish:** A patient's VMAT2 deficit or direct amphetamine versus methylphenidate clinical superiority.

**Related claims:** C-VMAT2-STRUCTURE (direct_model_supported); C-AMPH-EFFLUX (direct_model_supported); C-RELEASER-BLOCKER (specific_causal_inference_unsupported).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-55361-0 · PMID: 39747030 · PMCID: PMC11695631. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2023-AMPH-CALCIUM · 2023 · [Amphetamine-induced reverse transport of dopamine does not require cytosolic Ca2+](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10448275/)

**Study level:** primary cultured-neuron live imaging experiment — cultured dopaminergic neurons plus extracellular dopamine sensing cells.

**What was directly shown:** In cultured dopamine neurons amphetamine-evoked DAT-mediated efflux persisted despite calcium-channel blockade/chelation and action-potential suppression, while requiring DAT and VMAT2 function.

**What it cannot establish:** That neuronal calcium handling is abnormal in responders to dexamfetamine or that the effect translates into an individual's motor symptoms.

**Related claims:** C-AMPH-EFFLUX (direct_model_supported); C-RELEASER-BLOCKER (specific_causal_inference_unsupported).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1016/j.jbc.2023.105063 · PMID: 37468107 · PMCID: PMC10448275. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2026-PET-META · 2026 · [Pharmacological and non-pharmacological modulation of striatal dopamine release: a meta-analysis of [11C]raclopride PET studies](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03826-7)

**Study level:** human imaging meta-analysis — 92 PET studies / total n=1640, heterogeneous protocols.

**What was directly shown:** Both amphetamine and methylphenidate substantially increased striatal extracellular dopamine proxies in pooled human PET studies; approximate average changes were 10.9% vs 11.5% by [11C]raclopride binding measures.

**What it cannot establish:** Equality of clinical response, isolated tonic/phasic mechanisms, identical dosage routes or an individual's neurotransmitter lesion.

**Related claims:** C-RELEASER-BLOCKER (specific_causal_inference_unsupported).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1038/s41380-026-03826-7. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

## neuromuscular (1)

### P2020-BRODY · 2020 · [Clinical, morphological and genetic characterization of Brody disease: an international study of 40 patients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7009512/)

**Study level:** multinational human observational case cohort — 40 people with Brody disease.

**What was directly shown:** Exercise-induced stiffness, delayed relaxation, frequently preserved strength; electrically silent contractures in about half; reduced SERCA1 activity/protein.

**What it cannot establish:** All exercise stiffness is Brody disease, a universal SPG7-to-SERCA causal link, or that normal nerve conduction alone proves silent contracture.

**Related claims:** C-RELAXATION (human_clinical_supported_for_ATP2A1_disease).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1093/brain/awz410 · PMID: 32040565 · PMCID: PMC7009512. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

## autism (3)

### P2024-TOE-MOTOR · 2024 · [Toe walking in children and adolescents with Autism Spectrum Disorder: Relationship with sensory and motor functions, language, cognition, and autism severity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1750946724001326)

**Study level:** cross-sectional group comparison — 112 autistic participants; 61 with and 51 without toe walking.

**What was directly shown:** Toe-walking group had greater motor and developmental difficulties; Short Sensory Profile scores did not differ significantly between groups.

**What it cannot establish:** Absence of sensory differences in every individual, causation by calcium pumps, or inevitable progression with age.

**Related claims:** C-TOE-MECHANISMS (mixed_designs_no_causal_resolution).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1016/j.rasd.2024.102457. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2026-TOE-REVIEW · 2026 · [Comparative effectiveness of conservative and surgical interventions for toe walking in children with Autism Spectrum Disorder: a systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13430461/)

**Study level:** systematic review without pooled comparable effect estimate — 8 studies, 424 autistic participants, heterogeneous study designs/outcomes.

**What was directly shown:** Available intervention studies are highly heterogeneous and very-low-certainty; no reliable superiority ranking of treatment approaches can be made.

**What it cannot establish:** That multimodal therapy definitively outperforms surgery or any other intervention.

**Related claims:** C-TOE-MECHANISMS (mixed_designs_no_causal_resolution); C-TOE-TREATMENT (not_established).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.3389/fmed.2026.1832930 · PMID: 42549164 · PMCID: PMC13430461. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.

### P2026-TOE-SENSORY · 2026 · [Investigating the Relationship Between Sensory Processing, Pain and Toe Walking Gait: A Survey Study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jpc.70504)

**Study level:** online cross-sectional parent survey — 176 parents reporting on 187 children who toe-walk.

**What was directly shown:** Parents frequently reported sensory processing issues (98% within autistic toe-walking subgroup) and pain (48% of all toe-walking children) in this selected sample.

**What it cannot establish:** Sensory differences caused toe-walking; without a non-toe-walking comparison the high sensory prevalence cannot determine what distinguishes toe-walkers.

**Related claims:** C-TOE-MECHANISMS (mixed_designs_no_causal_resolution); C-TOE-PAIN (observational_association).

**Trace:** DOI: 10.1111/jpc.70504. **Archive:** link only pending rights/version check.
