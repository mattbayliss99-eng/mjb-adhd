# Question-first retrieval: enter without remembering the molecule

Choose the *function*, *failure*, or *observation* you can describe. Then follow the candidate processes, not an automatically inferred diagnosis.

## "An action can start, but it becomes progressively stiff and cannot reset"
- **Where?** Skeletal muscle contraction/relaxation (RyR1 → cytosolic Ca²⁺ → SERCA), nerve or descending motor drive, mechanical connective tissue.
- **Too little effective function:** reduced SERCA capacity; motor firing inability; inadequate ATP reserve.
- **Too much:** sustained central drive/co-contraction; excessive release relative to clearance.
- **Variable:** activity-dependent stiffening; insufficient time for recovery; pain and compensation.
- **Evidence:** P2020-BRODY; see C-RELAXATION.
- **Discriminator:** electrical activity persisting (EMG) versus mechanical tension persisting after activity; supervised assessment, not symptom-provocation alone.
- **What it cannot prove:** a specific genetic condition from normal strength or NCS findings.

## "Calcium is present, but the calcium response is unhelpful"
- **Where?** Outside cell (free ionised Ca²⁺), cytosol, SR/ER, mitochondrial matrix.
- **What matters?** Free calcium, bound/buffered calcium, entry rate, exit rate, membrane potential, calcium-phosphate buffering, energy demand.
- **Too little:** inadequate physiological pulse or uptake.
- **Too much:** excessive free calcium, ungated leak, organellar stress.
- **Variable:** poor response timing despite normal total Ca²⁺; source and destination mismatch.
- **Evidence:** P2017-EMRE, P2019-SPG7-MCU, P2026-MITO-REVIEW; claims C-SPG7-DIRECTION and C-MCU-ASSEMBLY.

## "Older sources say NCLX removes sodium-dependent mitochondrial calcium, newer sources disagree"
- **Locate the conflict:** P2025-NCLX-STRUCTURE vs P2025-TMEM65-EXCHANGER and P2025-TMEM65-NCLX-PARTNER.
- **Separate the claims:** transport exists; TMEM65 interacts with NCLX; isolated NCLX exchanges H+/Ca²⁺ in one assay; some groups show TMEM65-mediated Na+/Ca²⁺ exchange.
- **Do not collapse:** a cell-level dependency does not prove the dependent protein itself carries the ions.
- **2026 interpretation:** P2026-MITO-REVIEW explicitly calls the precise roles contentious. C-MITO-EFFLUX-IDENTITY.

## "Why would amphetamine help when a reuptake blocker differs?"
- **Source supply:** amino acid synthesis and vesicular proton gradient; P2025-VMAT2-STRUCTURE.
- **Release route:** action-potential/CaV-dependent vesicle exocytosis versus DAT-mediated efflux; P2023-AMPH-CALCIUM.
- **Human extracellular signal:** P2026-PET-META shows both amphetamine and methylphenidate produce substantial dopamine changes in PET datasets.
- **Alternative levels:** noradrenaline, TAAR1, dose/exposure kinetics, receptor adaptations, motor network selection, pain/energy changes.
- **Do not infer:** lesion localisation, VMAT2 deficiency or spontaneous recovery from a subjective drug contrast. C-RELEASER-BLOCKER.

## "Is persistent toe-walking due to sensory discomfort?"
- **Different comparison designs:** P2024-TOE-MOTOR directly compares autistic toe-walkers and autistic non-toe-walkers; P2026-TOE-SENSORY surveys children who toe-walk and reports frequent sensory difficulty.
- **Other candidate causes:** motor planning, tone, passive shortening, dorsiflexion, balance, reflexes, dystonia, feedback control.
- **Too little / too much:** reduced selective motor control; excessive tone or protective contraction; fixed biomechanical shortening.
- **Implications:** clinical assessment before treatment attribution; age-related motor deterioration is **not** inferred merely from toe-walking.
- **Interventions:** P2026-TOE-REVIEW states no treatment superiority established.

## "A signal appears normal, but function is absent"
- **Measurement matters:** normal nerve conduction does not rule out SERCA failure, central motor dysfunction, small-fibre dysfunction or mitochondrial pathology.
- **Probe the stages:** component availability → maturation → compartment → assembly → gating → useful output → clearance → repeatability.
- **Return to:** C-RELAXATION, C-MCU-ASSEMBLY, C-SPG7-AXON and the claim-level transfer caveats.
