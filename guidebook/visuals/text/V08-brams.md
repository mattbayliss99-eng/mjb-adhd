# V08 Brams et al. 2012 (R1)

Randomised withdrawal, six weeks. Adults stable on lisdexamfetamine for at least six months.

Placebo: 45/60 (75%) met relapse criteria.
Continued treatment: 5/56 (8.9%) met relapse criteria.

Symptom relapse in previously stable adult responders. Does not measure death, permanent injury, or the effect of every treatment interruption. Not every patient relapses. Figures taken from the guidebook claim; the full paper tables were not re-read in this pass.
