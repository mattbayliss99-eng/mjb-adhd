# V04 three definitions

Dystonia (N1): involuntary contractions and postures. Ask which part, which task, rest or action.
Spasticity (N2): increased tone after nervous-system injury or disease. Ask whether the problem is resistance, a sudden spasm, or a fixed restriction.
Ataxia (N3): difficulty coordinating movement. Ask whether the example is a misjudged reach, loss of balance, or weakness.

Comparison for assessment. Not a self-diagnosis. Not proposed as Matthew's diagnoses.
