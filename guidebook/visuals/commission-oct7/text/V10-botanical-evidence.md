# V10 three evidence cards

F1 Baziar 2019: six-week double-blind saffron versus methylphenidate pilot, 54 children and adolescents. Does not establish adult efficacy, equivalence, or selective reuptake action.

F2/F3: Mucuna trials in Parkinson disease, different stages and designs. Not an ADHD trial. No Mucuna-plus-saffron ADHD trial was reviewed.

F4 Cohen 2022: levodopa content of commercial Mucuna supplements varied. A sampled product does not establish another supplier or an untested batch. Product-quality evidence and clinical-effectiveness evidence are separate.

No dose instructions. No interchangeability claim.
