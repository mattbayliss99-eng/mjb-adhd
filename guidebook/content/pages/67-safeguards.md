@layer 1 Rules need to change care
!guidance(ACC-CQC) CQC’s fundamental standards cover safety, person-centred care, safeguarding, complaints, governance and candour.

An available enforcement power and an improvement in a patient’s care are different facts. Ask for the decision record and the evidence of changed practice.
