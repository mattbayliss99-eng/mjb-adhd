@layer 1 Quick understanding
Every important sentence carries a label. Tap it to see the sources. A page can mix labels — a patient observation next to a research finding next to a hypothesis — and each keeps its own status.

@component legend

@layer 2 Three separate questions
1. **What kind of claim is it?** — the label.
2. **How strong is the evidence?** — confidence: strong, moderate, limited or pending.
3. **What did we actually read?** — access: full text, abstract, supplied document, or pointer only. A strong study read only in abstract is still marked as abstract.

@layer 3 How to use it
- Turn labels off with the **Labels** button for a clean read; turn them on to check.
- Use the **depth dial** to stop at the level you need.
- Hover any internal link for a preview; the map beside each page shows what connects to it.

@layer 5 Release states
Pages move through: proposed → drafted → sourced → medically/editorially reviewed → integrated → checked → published. The current state is shown under each title.

@uncertainty
Labels are editorial judgements and can be wrong; the corrections log records changes.
