@layer 1 Quick understanding
!guidance(N4) Sensory neuropathy can impair awareness of hand or foot position and contribute to poor balance or coordination. That is one possible explanation, not the only one.


@visual V03

@layer 2 Possible explanations
!guidance(N9) Developmental coordination disorder can affect everyday coordination. A new or changing movement problem also needs assessment for other neurological causes rather than an assumed developmental explanation.

@layer 3 Useful next action
Describe one ordinary task: what you intended, what visibly happened, and what you could feel. Ask for position-sense, strength and coordination assessment. Do not use unsafe balance challenges as home tests.

@layer 4 Treatment logic
!guidance(N9) Physiotherapy and occupational therapy can help with function and practical task adaptations. Support should address the actual difficulties identified.

@layer 5 Deeper explanation
A patient can report intense sensation alongside unclear movement feedback. That observation does not, by itself, establish a neurotransmitter deficit, blocked telemetry or a specific nerve lesion.
