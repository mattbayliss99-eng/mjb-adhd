@layer 1 Quick understanding
!guidance(N2) Spasticity and spasms can limit movement, comfort and daily activities. Weakness and muscle shortening can also contribute to stiffness.

@layer 2 Possible explanations
Use concrete language: resistance when moving, a sudden spasm, a fixed restriction, or difficulty generating movement. Ask the examiner which findings explain each problem.

@layer 3 Useful next action
List activities affected, positions that help, triggers and changes over time. Ask for an assessment of tone, strength and joint range, and a plan for transfers or personal care if these are difficult.

@layer 4 Treatment logic
!guidance(N2) Management may combine positioning, physiotherapy and medicines. Treatments should be chosen around the individual and their goals rather than the amount of stiffness alone.

@layer 5 Deeper explanation
Ask what the proposed intervention targets and how comfort and useful function will be assessed. Record adverse effects as well as any change in spasms.

@uncertainty
Feeling stiff does not confirm spasticity or its cause. This page does not recommend an unsupervised stretching or drug regimen.
