@layer 1 Quick understanding
!guidance(N8) Persistent fatigue can have several causes, including disrupted sleep, medicines and medical conditions. It should not automatically be attributed to ADHD.

@layer 2 Possible explanations
!guidance(N8) Loud snoring with gasping or choking during sleep, persistent thirst, weight change or breathlessness are examples of associated symptoms that can guide assessment.

@layer 3 Useful next action
Keep a short record of sleep opportunity, awakenings, daytime sleepiness and activities limited by fatigue. Bring medication and substance timing. Ask whether examination or tests are appropriate.

@layer 4 Treatment logic
!guidance(N8) Seek a GP assessment when fatigue persists for weeks without a clear reason or interferes with daily life. Treatment depends on the cause.

@layer 5 Deeper explanation
The aim is a useful plan, not a perfect diary. Choose two or three observations you can repeat without exhausting yourself.
