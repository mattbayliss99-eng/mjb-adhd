@layer 1 Build your sheet
Clinicians have little time. A sheet that leads with the decision you need, then three concrete examples, gets further than a long history. Nothing you type is stored or sent.

@component appointment-builder

@layer 3 Useful next action
- [ ] Lead with the one decision you need
- [ ] Bring exact medicine names, manufacturers and strengths
- [ ] Bring three dated examples, not general descriptions
- [ ] Ask for the plan in writing before you leave

@uncertainty
A good sheet improves communication; it cannot guarantee a particular decision.
