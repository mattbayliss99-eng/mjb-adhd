# The science and the system, side by side (draft)

This draft pairs what basic science establishes with what the care system says it provides. The aim is to explain why timely assessment and continuity matter. A study of how dopamine works does not create a legal duty, and it does not predict any one person's clinical outcome. Each row keeps the two columns separate.

Status key: **checked** means read against the cited source for this draft. **HOLD** means the claim must be verified against the primary source before publication.
