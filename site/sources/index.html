Evidence and provenance

Source documents

These are dated copies of policies used to understand routes and institutional duties. They may have been replaced. Check the issuing organisation for the current version before relying on a procedure or deadline.

Policy copies

  • CWPT Complaints Policy, version 9.1 (PDF) — historical supplied copy; verify current complaints procedure.
  • CWPT Safeguarding Adults Policy, version 5.0 (PDF) — historical supplied copy; it does not determine an individual's safeguarding threshold.
  • CWPT Freedom to Speak Up Policy, version 6.5 (PDF) — a staff speaking-up route, not a patient complaints channel.
  • Coventry and Warwickshire ICB ADHD under-25 commissioning policy, version 3 (PDF) — historical copy. It should not be presented as the current referral policy.
