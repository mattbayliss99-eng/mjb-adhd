Blood flow oxygen and glucose delivery

Oxygen and glucose have different delivery and membrane-entry routes. Reduced delivery and impaired cellular use are different hypotheses.

What the diagram shows

Blood carries oxygen, largely on haemoglobin, and glucose in solution. Oxygen leaves blood and diffuses through tissue; glucose uses transport systems including GLUT1 at the blood-brain barrier. The respiratory chain uses oxygen as a terminal electron acceptor. Transport/delivery limitations and defects of intracellular metabolism are different possibilities requiring their own evidence. ADHD alone demonstrates neither. This corrected diagram removes the implication that oxygen crosses through GLUT1.
