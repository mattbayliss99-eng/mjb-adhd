Large scale brain networks

Connectivity measures describe relationships in recorded brain activity. Dashed lines show statistical dependence, not axons or proven causal influence.

What the diagram shows

Functional connectivity quantifies statistical relationships between measured signals. It is distinct from an anatomical tract and from a demonstrated causal effect. ADHD studies examine group-level relationships among default-mode, attention, control and other networks; results vary with age, sample, task, motion and analysis. The network layout is conceptual, not a coordinate map or a faithful reproduction of a particular study result. Neither connectivity nor one network measure supplies a stand-alone diagnosis.
