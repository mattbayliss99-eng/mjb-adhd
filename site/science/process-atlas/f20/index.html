One carbon metabolism and methyl donors

One-carbon transfer and SAM-dependent methylation are linked processes. A biochemical pathway is not a personalised supplement prescription.

What the diagram shows

Folate and cobalamin-dependent methionine synthase link one-carbon metabolism to methionine regeneration. SAM formed from methionine serves as a methyl donor in many reactions, including COMT and PNMT. After methyl donation, SAH is processed to homocysteine, which has several metabolic fates. This diagram omits intermediate steps and other folate functions. A common MTHFR variant alone does not demonstrate failed methylation, ADHD causation or benefit from a specific supplement.
