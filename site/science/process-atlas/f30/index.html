Histamine and orexin arousal modules

Histamine and orexin are distinct systems with interacting arousal roles. Neither system supplies a one-to-one map to an ADHD symptom.

What the diagram shows

Histamine is a monoamine, while orexin/hypocretin molecules are peptides. Rodent experiments distinguish their roles in wakefulness and demonstrate orexin excitation of histamine neurons. Both operate in wider interacting arousal systems. The schematic separates origins, transmitters and evidence scope; it is not a complete anatomical map, evidence of a deficit in the reader or a rationale for an ADHD medicine or supplement.
