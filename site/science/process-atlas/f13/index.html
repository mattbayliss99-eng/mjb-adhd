Vitamin and mineral support

A required cofactor is not evidence that extra intake will help. Cofactor requirement does not establish deficiency or a supplement benefit.

What the diagram shows

Enzymes require specific cofactors or co-substrates: TH and tryptophan hydroxylase use iron and BH4; AADC uses pyridoxal phosphate; DBH uses copper and ascorbate; PNMT and COMT use SAM as a methyl donor. These examples explain chemistry. They do not show that a person is deficient, that extra intake increases transmitter function, or that a supplement treats ADHD. No doses or personalised supplement recommendations are implied.
