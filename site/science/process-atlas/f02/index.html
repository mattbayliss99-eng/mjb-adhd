Amino acid supply and brain entry

Supply, transport and synthesis are separate control points. A blood level or dietary increase does not directly measure a synapse.

What the diagram shows

Amino acids are supplied by diet and protein turnover. Brain entry is regulated by transporters, including LAT1, which transports several large neutral amino acids as part of an exchange system. Circulating abundance, transport competition, intracellular supply, enzyme activity and transmitter handling must be distinguished. More dietary precursor does not guarantee more transmitter released at a relevant synapse. The LAT1 experimental source includes mouse studies and human rare-variant observations, not a dietary ADHD treatment trial.
