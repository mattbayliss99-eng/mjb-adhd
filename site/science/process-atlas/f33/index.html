DBH and PNMT compartment detail

DBH acts in secretory vesicles; PNMT acts in cytosol of expressing cells. These branches require the relevant enzymes; not every neuron has both.

What the diagram shows

DBH is located in secretory vesicles, including noradrenergic neurons and chromaffin cells. It uses copper and ascorbate in conversion of dopamine to noradrenaline. In PNMT-expressing cells, noradrenaline is available in cytosol for SAM-dependent methylation to adrenaline, which can then be repackaged. The paired compartments correct the impression that both enzymes act freely in the same space or that every dopamine cell produces adrenaline.
