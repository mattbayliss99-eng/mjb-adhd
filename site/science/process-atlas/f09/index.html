Dopamine and noradrenaline projection systems

Selected projection examples: schematic routes, not anatomical positions. Projection systems are not one-to-one maps from a transmitter to a symptom.

What the diagram shows

Different dopamine cell populations project to cortex, limbic regions and striatum. Locus-coeruleus populations provide noradrenergic projections to cortical and other targets and are not a completely uniform system. This deliberately non-anatomical route map names selected examples, not every origin, target or collateral. Tracing studies cited include rodent and primate work; the simplified route names do not imply the complete architecture is identical across species or that one pathway uniquely causes an ADHD symptom.
