Energy supply and neuronal demand

Neurons use energy to maintain gradients and support signalling. This is normal physiology, not proof of an energy defect in ADHD.

What the diagram shows

Aerobic metabolism supplies reducing equivalents to the respiratory chain. Proton pumping and ATP synthase couple electron transfer to ATP generation. Cells use ATP for ion homeostasis, biosynthesis and many aspects of signalling. Glycolysis also produces ATP, and fuel contributions differ with cell and context. Mitochondria do not directly manufacture dopamine. An explanation of energy supply and demand does not establish mitochondrial dysfunction or an energy deficit in an individual with ADHD.
