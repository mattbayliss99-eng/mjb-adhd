Adrenal hormones: two tissue compartments

Cortisol and adrenaline come from different adrenal tissues. Peripheral stress hormones cannot be read as a brain dopamine measurement.

What the diagram shows

The adrenal cortex makes cortisol through steroid biosynthesis, while medullary chromaffin cells make catecholamines through enzymes including DBH and PNMT. Their tissue origins, chemistry and regulation differ. The simplified lanes illustrate these distinctions, not the complete HPA or autonomic feedback networks. Blood catecholamine concentrations cannot be equated with transmitter levels at a brain synapse, and the diagram does not demonstrate a cortisol abnormality or a specific stress cause of ADHD.
