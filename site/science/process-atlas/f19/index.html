Redox balance and glutathione

Glutathione participates in redox chemistry; balance matters. This normal redox system does not establish oxidative stress as an ADHD cause.

What the diagram shows

Glutathione synthesis combines glutamate and cysteine, then glycine, in ATP-requiring steps. Peroxidase reactions can oxidise reduced glutathione to GSSG while reducing peroxides. Glutathione reductase uses NADPH to regenerate GSH. Reactive species have normal signalling roles as well as damaging effects when regulation fails. The cycle is an educational simplification and not evidence that an individual has oxidative stress, requires an antioxidant or has ADHD because of a redox abnormality.
