Astrocytes and transmitter recycling

Astrocytes support transmitter handling and the cellular environment. Amino-acid recycling is different from recycling a vesicle membrane.

What the diagram shows

The glutamate–glutamine shuttle links astrocytic uptake and glutamine synthesis with neuronal glutamine uptake and glutaminase activity. This is one important route, not the entire metabolism of either cell. Astrocytes also support the extracellular environment and exchange metabolic substrates. The figure does not designate a lactate shuttle as the only neuronal fuel route, identify a patient defect or equate amino-acid recycling with synaptic-vesicle recycling.
