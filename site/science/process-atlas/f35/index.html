PFC receptor signalling in a defined context

A specific experimental context: prefrontal working-memory circuits. More stimulation does not mean more benefit in every context.

What the diagram shows

Primate prefrontal experiments support alpha2A-associated regulation of cAMP/HCN signalling and recurrent activity, and non-linear D1 effects on neurons engaged in working memory. These are defined cellular/task contexts. A conceptual inverted-U result is not a measured curve for an individual, a plasma-level target, a universal effect across brain regions or a justification for increasing medication. The figure deliberately avoids numerical concentrations and dose recommendations.
