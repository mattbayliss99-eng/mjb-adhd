Immune signalling and neural context

Immune signalling and neural context are research questions, not a universal cause. Established immune biology is not proof that ADHD is universally inflammatory.

What the diagram shows

Innate immune receptor cascades describe established cellular signalling. Microglia also have roles in brain development and responses to injury; a cited mouse experiment investigated synaptic pruning. Those observations do not demonstrate the same mechanism in every human or establish an inflammatory or autoimmune cause of ADHD. Any ADHD-specific immune claim needs its own human study, phenotype, direction-of-effect and confounding assessment. The figure therefore separates normal biological context from an unproven clinical hypothesis.
