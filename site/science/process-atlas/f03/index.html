Catecholamine synthesis and cell type branches

The catecholamine branch depends on which enzymes a cell expresses. Not every dopamine-producing neuron makes noradrenaline or adrenaline.

What the diagram shows

Tyrosine hydroxylase forms L-DOPA in the cytosol; aromatic L-amino acid decarboxylase forms dopamine. VMAT2 can transport dopamine into secretory vesicles. In cells expressing dopamine beta-hydroxylase, dopamine is converted to noradrenaline inside those vesicles, using copper and ascorbate. In cells expressing PNMT, noradrenaline returns to the cytosol for SAM-dependent conversion to adrenaline, which can be repackaged. These are cell-specific branches, not a compulsory chain in every dopamine neuron. Dopamine is the same molecule in peripheral and brain compartments.
