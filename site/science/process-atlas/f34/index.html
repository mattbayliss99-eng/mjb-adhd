DAT uptake and ion gradients

DAT transport uses ion gradients; ATP maintains those gradients indirectly. DAT is not directly powered by ATP hydrolysis.

What the diagram shows

DAT cotransports dopamine with sodium and chloride across the plasma membrane. Its transport energetics depend on electrochemical gradients; the Na+/K+ ATPase supports ionic gradients using ATP but DAT does not itself act as an ATP-hydrolysing pump. Intracellular dopamine can be stored or metabolised. NET can contribute to dopamine clearance in regions with low DAT, supported by regional experimental evidence. This is not a genotype-based inference about the reader.
