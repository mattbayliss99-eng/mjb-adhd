Acetylcholine and attention modulation

Acetylcholine is made, released, hydrolysed and its choline reused. DAT and MAO are not the main acetylcholine clearance pathway.

What the diagram shows

Choline acetyltransferase synthesises acetylcholine from choline and acetyl-CoA. Vesicular transport and calcium-dependent exocytosis support release. Acetylcholinesterase, associated with extracellular membrane surfaces, rapidly hydrolyses the transmitter, and choline uptake supplies material for reuse. Nicotinic and muscarinic receptors act differently. Acetylcholine participates in many central and peripheral functions; a mechanism cartoon does not imply an attention-specific defect or justify an individual treatment.
