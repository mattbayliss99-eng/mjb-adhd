Medication absorption metabolism and elimination

Drug exposure is not the same thing as synaptic transmitter metabolism. These steps do not supply a dose or justify changing a prescribed regimen.

What the diagram shows

Absorption, distribution, metabolism and elimination describe pharmacokinetics. They differ between medicines and formulations. For dexamfetamine, the current UK product information describes gastrointestinal absorption, distribution including brain entry, biotransformation and urinary elimination. This general diagram does not specify a concentration, dose or metabolic genotype interpretation. Clinical effect and tolerability require assessment and monitoring; systemic drug handling must not be conflated with MAO/COMT handling of dopamine.
