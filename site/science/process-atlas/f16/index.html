Serotonin supply and transmission

Brain and peripheral serotonin are separate compartments. Serotonin is not simply a happiness chemical; tissue context matters.

What the diagram shows

Tryptophan hydroxylation requires iron, BH4 and oxygen. TPH2 is the key neuronal isoform in brain serotonin synthesis, while TPH1 operates in many peripheral serotonin-producing tissues. AADC then forms serotonin, which is stored and released in the neuronal compartment. SERT-dependent uptake contributes to clearance. These compartments should not be joined by an arrow suggesting peripheral serotonin directly supplies brain synapses. The pathways alone do not predict mood or treatment response.
