Glutamate transmission

Glutamate transmission combines vesicle loading, receptors and uptake. An excitatory transmitter pathway is not a measure of ADHD severity.

What the diagram shows

Presynaptic vesicular glutamate transporters load glutamate for release. Postsynaptic ionotropic and metabotropic receptors have different effects and kinetics. Extracellular glutamate is cleared by excitatory amino-acid transporters, including important astrocytic routes. Glutamine synthetase uses ATP to form glutamine, which can return to neurons. This is established neurotransmission; it does not diagnose excessive excitation, a glutamate defect or a particular cause of ADHD.
