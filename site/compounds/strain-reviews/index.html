Strain & chemovar reviews

Last updated 1 October 2026

Personal reviews of prescribed cannabis products. Each review keeps two boxes apart: what was experienced, and what measured product data and independent pharmacology actually support.

Review rule. The second box never inherits claims just because they appear in the first. Measured cannabinoid/terpene composition beats strain-name assumptions. Every review is N=1 unless stated otherwise.

Initial review candidates

Cookies / Girl Scout Cookies family

WHAT I EXPERIENCED

Among the strongest personal-response profiles in the source guide.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE / PRODUCT DATA SUPPORT

Publish product/batch response separately from general terpene-family interpretation.

Gelato / Mochi Gelato

WHAT I EXPERIENCED

Used as a calmer, overflow-state example.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE / PRODUCT DATA SUPPORT

MGT T28 Mochi Gelato product image held. Keep product-specific measured data separate from generic Gelato-family assumptions.

Green Crack

WHAT I EXPERIENCED

Useful personal daytime/activation response.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE / PRODUCT DATA SUPPORT

N=1 until product/batch chemistry is available.

Durban Poison

WHAT I EXPERIENCED

Distinctive clear, functional response; THCV/pinene hypotheses in the notes.

WHAT THE EVIDENCE / PRODUCT DATA SUPPORT

No specific chemistry attributed to a product without laboratory data.

Kush / Chemango Kush

WHAT I EXPERIENCED

WHAT THE EVIDENCE / PRODUCT DATA SUPPORT

Separate cultivar-family expectations from measured batch chemistry.
