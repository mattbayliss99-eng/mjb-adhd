  1. W1 NICE. Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: diagnosis and management (NG87). 2018, updated 2019. Recommendations 1.7.11–1.7.14 (adult medication choice) and section 1.10 (review and discontinuation). Source Official text via search snippets and NHS shared-care documents quoting it; main page fetch blocked

    Guidance shapes practice; it does not create an enforceable right to a particular medicine.

  2. W2 Li L, Zhu N, Zhang L, et al. ADHD pharmacotherapy and mortality in individuals with ADHD. JAMA 2024;331(10):850–860. Sweden, n=148,578, ages 6–64. Source Abstract and key results read (PubMed); full text open access

    Compares starting medication within 3 months of diagnosis with not starting. It is not a study of stopping an established treatment.
