ADHD resources: find what you need

Status: drafted · Content checked 2026-10-07

Purpose-labelled links for medication questions, shared care, peer support, work, books, podcasts and practical tools.

1 · Choose your purpose

You do not need to read everything. Pick the question you have today, open one resource, and keep anything useful with your appointment notes.

This directory brings together public resources from ADHD Direct’s recommendations, with additional official links where they help. Every entry explains its purpose and what kind of source it is. No personal clinical correspondence is reproduced here.

  • Medication or shared care: start with the NHS overview, the clinic explainers and the exact product leaflet.
  • Day-to-day support: try one practical video, support group or simple tool.
  • Work: check the official Access to Work route.
  • Longer reading or listening: choose a book or podcast by topic, format and energy available.

Source type: Peer support. Scottish support network; group availability and remit vary.

## AADD-UK Useful for: Explore adult ADHD peer resources and UK support-group information.

Source type: Peer support. An adult ADHD community resource; check the date of individual pages and current group details.

Source type: Practical information. US-based resource. Its healthcare, employment and education rules are not UK rules.

## Access to Work Useful for: Check the support available for disability-related difficulties at work and how to apply.

Source type: Government scheme. Official guidance for England, Scotland and Wales. Eligibility and awarded support are assessed; Northern Ireland has a separate route.

Open this resource · RES-ATW

## ADHD and driving — GOV.UK Useful for: Check the current DVLA notification guidance rather than relying on a social-media answer.

Source type: Government guidance. Official Great Britain guidance; use the linked rules for your licence category and circumstances.

Open this resource · RES-DRIVING

## ADDitude Useful for: Browse articles, expert discussions and practical ADHD resources.

Source type: Magazine / educational content. US-based commercial publication with advertising and paid material. Publication does not make every article a clinical guideline.

Open this resource · RES-ADD

4 · Books: know what you are choosing

Book links identify the title and author. They are not “must-buy” endorsements or claims that the whole text has been independently reviewed. Check library availability, an excerpt or an accessible format before buying.

## Taking Charge of Adult ADHD — Russell A. Barkley, with Christine M. Benton Useful for: Explore a structured reference on adult ADHD, treatment questions and practical strategies.

Source type: Book. Second edition verified on the publisher’s page; paperback ISBN 9781462546855. A listing check is not a full review of the book.

Open this resource · RES-BARKLEY

## Driven to Distraction — Edward M. Hallowell and John J. Ratey Useful for: Explore an established ADHD book through the author’s bibliography.

Source type: Book. Book listing checked on the author’s site. Check edition and date when discussing treatment information.

Open this resource · RES-HALLOWELL

## Scattered Minds — Gabor Maté Useful for: Explore the author’s perspective on attention difficulties and lived experience.

Source type: Book / author perspective. Listed in the supplied resource recommendations. An author’s explanatory framework is not established proof of ADHD causation; compare claims with the genetics and evidence routes.

Open this resource · RES-MATE

## Atomic Habits — James Clear Useful for: Explore approaches to building routines and changing the surrounding environment.

Source type: General self-help book. General habit advice, not an ADHD-specific clinical reference or evidence that disability can be overcome by willpower.

Open this resource · RES-HABITS

5 · Podcasts and practical tools

Apps support a task or routine; they are not treatments in themselves. Pricing, permissions and accessibility can change. Choose a format that works for you.

## The ADHD Adults Podcast Useful for: Listen to UK adult ADHD discussion, information and personal experiences.

Source type: Podcast. Podcast format; individual episodes mix explanation and lived experience.

Open this resource · RES-POD-ADULT

## ADDitude ADHD Experts Podcast Useful for: Find topic-specific expert discussions, episodes and accompanying slides.

Source type: Webinars / podcast. Publisher’s indexed listing verified through search; direct page access was blocked during this check. Check each speaker, date and any sponsorship.

Open this resource · RES-POD-ADD

## Forest Useful for: Try a visual focus timer for a short task.

Source type: Focus app. Optional commercial tool; check current platform, pricing and accessibility before installing.

Open this resource · RES-FOREST

## Todoist Useful for: Keep tasks and reminders in one place.

Source type: Task app. Optional organisation tool; start with a small list rather than an elaborate system.

Open this resource · RES-TODOIST

## SimpleMind Useful for: Arrange ideas visually when a linear list is difficult to use.

Source type: Mind-mapping app. Optional commercial tool; check your device, export options and accessibility.

Open this resource · RES-SIMPLEMIND

## Productive Useful for: Try reminders and a simple record of a routine.

Source type: Habit app. Optional commercial tool. Tracking should reduce effort rather than become another obligation.

Open this resource · RES-PRODUCTIVE

6 · Use a resource in your next appointment

Turn what you read into one concrete question: what needs deciding, what information is missing, and who will do the next step? Use Prepare for an appointment, Transfers, shared care and Right to Choose and How evidence is labelled.

Uncertainty

Links and bibliographic details were checked on 7 October 2026. A working link does not establish the quality of every statement on an external site. NHS and government sources, provider explanations, personal experience and commercial resources have different roles. New recommendations need an identifiable publisher or listing before they are added.
