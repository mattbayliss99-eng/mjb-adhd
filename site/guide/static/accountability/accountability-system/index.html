Who is responsible?

Status: drafted · Content checked 2026-10-07

Separate the next care decision from complaints, regulation and investigation.

Start with ownership

A complaint acknowledgment does not itself produce care. Ask which clinician or service owns the next decision, what the decision is, when it will happen and what protects you while you wait.

England’s local service complaints usually belong with the provider or ICB; NHS England has a defined remit, rather than responsibility for every complaint.

ACC-NHSE

Useful next action

Keep two requests visible: the care you need now, and the investigation of what went wrong. Ask for a named owner and deadline for each.

Understand the gap

HSSIB identified unclear responsibility across organisational boundaries and transitions. A handover needs accepted responsibility, not just a sent referral.
