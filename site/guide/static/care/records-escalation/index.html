Records and escalation

Status: drafted · Content checked 2026-10-06

Four separate routes — clinical decision, complaint, your records, safeguarding — each with its own owner and clock. Use the right one for each question.

1 · Quick understanding

QuestionRouteWho answers
Will I get treatment, and when?Clinical requestThe treating service or prescriber
Was I treated properly?Complaint (then the Ombudsman)The provider's complaints team
What does my record say?Subject access requestThe organisation's records or information team
Is someone at risk of harm now?Safeguarding concernThe local authority safeguarding team, with health partners

2 · Possible explanations

Things stall when routes are mixed: a clinical question gets an answer from the complaints team; a records request gets sent to the Freedom of Information route; a safeguarding concern is filed as a complaint.

Your own personal information is requested through a subject access request under UK GDPR, normally answered within one calendar month. Freedom of Information is for information about an organisation, not your own records.

L1

In one case a trust clinician directed a patient's request for his own records to the Freedom of Information process; the correct route is a subject access request.

4 · Treatment logic

One trust's safeguarding policy includes "an avoidable deterioration in physical or mental health" in its definition of significant harm, and lists the Human Rights Act articles on life, freedom from degrading treatment and private life as underpinning its duty.

P2

A definition is a starting point, not a verdict. Statutory safeguarding duties also depend on whether the person has care and support needs, is experiencing or at risk of abuse or neglect, and cannot protect themselves. Check the current version of any policy before relying on it.

The same trust's complaints policy sets out its own process and timescales; its stated review date was 7 July 2026, so check the current version for later events.

P4

Uncertainty

Policies change; the versions cited here were read in October 2026 and may have been superseded.
