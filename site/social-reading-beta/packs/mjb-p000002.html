Move at your own pace

Each post has a stable address. Previous and Next work without swiping or JavaScript.

Layout prototype — not proven to suit everyone with ADHD.

Buttons first, swipe optional

Visible Previous and Next are always enough. Optional horizontal swipe starts off. Vertical scrolling, text selection and browser Back stay native.

Share a specific step

Every explanation step has a stable ID. Share links open that step directly. Ordinary browsing does not flood browser history with every step.

Caveats stay with the claim

Qualifications sit on the same step as the statement they qualify. A later evidence panel cannot repair a misleading earlier slide.

This layout is a prototype being tested. It is not proven to suit everyone with ADHD.
