Your no-obligation journal

Dates and times are added automatically. Write as much or as little as you want. Change the title and colours whenever you like.

How dates, storage and privacy work

Each entry records when you first saved it and your device time zone automatically. Editing keeps that original date and adds a separate edited date. You never have to fill anything in.

Entries are kept only in this browser's local storage. That storage is not encrypted, is not synced, and can be removed by browser cleanup or private browsing. This page never sends journal text to MJB, analytics, AI services, logs or cloud accounts. Files only leave the browser when you choose Download, Copy, Share or Print.

Optional name

Adds your name to the title. Skip keeps the default. No account; stored only here.

Keep a copy

Copy, Share, Markdown and Print use the selected entries, or all entries when none are selected.

Stored journal data is unreadable. Saving is paused so nothing is overwritten.
