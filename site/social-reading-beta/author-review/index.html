They are not a diagnostic test and they do not tell a clinician which medication to use (Tier B).CLM-GEN-01

MJB-P000003MJB-S000003-01The dopamine pathway, step by stepThe short versionThis is a map of how one dopamine-releasing nerve cell in the brain makes, stores and releases dopamine, and of what then happens to the dopamine. MJB-P000003MJB-S000003-02The dopamine pathway, step by stepMain line: inside a brain dopamine cellTyrosine in the blood is carried into the brain by LAT1. Tyrosine hydroxylase needs BH4 to work. Tyrosine hydroxylase's product, L-DOPA, is converted to dopamine by AADC. Newly made dopamine is loaded into vesicles by VMAT2. Filled vesicles release dopamine when calcium enters the nerve ending.CLM-PATH-01
