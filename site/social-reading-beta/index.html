What is ADHD?

ADHD is a recognised neurodevelopmental condition described by persistent patterns of inattention and/or hyperactivity and impulsivity with functional impact.

A diagnosis requires a structured assessment; a single behaviour, questionnaire score or gene result does not establish it.
