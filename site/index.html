Freedom · Dignity · Accountability · Continuity · Evidence

My journey. And the ridiculousness of it.

Five years on a plan. Then the plan stopped. Coventry and Warwickshire Partnership NHS Trust and Coventry and Warwickshire Integrated Care Board still have not put it back. The science door is the evidence. The journey door is the count. This is advocacy, not medical advice.

⚠️ For your own safety

Keep all correspondence and expect nothing. Save every letter, email, text and appointment note. Write down what was said, by whom, and when.

You can only rely on family and friends to support you. Currently the NHS is not reliable. Do not assume a service will catch you because it says it exists to help.

Too many people die because of people who could not care less. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, call 999. For urgent help that is not an emergency, call NHS 111, or Samaritans on 116 123 (free, 24 hours).

👇🏻 Show more: get it in writing

Text SHOUT to 85258, free, 24/7. Some NHS areas use their own keyword with the same number; check your local trust's website. Say in writing what is happening and the effect your treatment, or the lack of it, is having on you.

Can't make voice calls? Register for emergencySMS now by texting register to 999. It is for people who are deaf, hard of hearing or speech impaired, and must be set up before you need it.

Record everything. Every call, message and letter with the NHS or emergency services: date, time, who, what they said they would do. Follow calls up in writing. Never assume people will do what they say. At worst, you have a dated record that people were told and did not act.
