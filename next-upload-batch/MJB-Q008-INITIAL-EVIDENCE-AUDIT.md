| P01, P03, P06 | Romigi et al., 2020, DOI 10.3389/fneur.2020.00713. HRV in 22 video-EEG-confirmed functional-seizure patients, 11 without and 11 with coexisting epilepsy. Peri-event autonomic variation reported. | **Indirect support only** for physiological state transitions; HRV is NOT a dopamine, noradrenaline or metabolic-resource assay. | P01, P06, P08 | Pick et al., *Autonomic, Endocrine, and Inflammation Profiles in Functional Neurological Disorder* (2022), DOI 10.1176/appi.neuropsych.21010025. | Broad FND systematic review finds heterogeneity, inconsistent HRV metrics and uncertain biomarker discrimination. Counters a universal autonomic signature. It does not eliminate possible mechanistically distinct subgroups. | Extract results by FDS phenotype, sampling time, outcome, comparator, within-person vs between-person; examine negative studies. |
| P05, P04 | *Functional seizure semiology and classification in a public and private hospital*, Seizure 2024, DOI 10.1016/j.seizure.2024.09.020. Post-event confusion/fatigue reported commonly (~58%). | Supports the importance of collecting recovery *symptoms*, not a mechanism or prescribed fixed recovery time. Does not identify ATP depletion, catecholamine shifts, or reserve restoration as causes. | Prospective quantified recovery duration across autonomic, attention, motor, fatigue, pain metrics. |
| P02, P07, P11 | Xue et al., *Subjective symptoms of functional/dissociative seizures and their diagnostic value*, Epilepsia 2026, DOI 10.1002/epi.70143. Systematic review of 47 studies: sensory, arousal, emotional, consciousness, cognitive, motor symptoms, with considerable heterogeneity. | Shows broad phenotypic variability, **not** selective impairment of any proposed dopamine-associated resource-dependent circuit. Supports careful subgroup design, and undermines post-hoc universal mechanism claims. | Task-level longitudinal studies with comparator groups, matched cognitive/motor load and separately measured circuitry/resource variables. |
| P06, P09 | Bakvis et al., *Basal hypercortisolism and trauma in patients with PNES*, Epilepsia 2010, PMID 19889016. 18 PNES, 19 controls. Cortisol differences in subgroup; salivary alpha-amylase sampled. Other stress-physiology studies found no systematic group cortisol changes. | HPA-axis findings can differ by cohort; cortisol and salivary alpha-amylase are not proof of a CNS dopamine/noradrenaline switching mechanism. | Directly measure relevant candidate molecules at validated sites/times; distinguish group stress history vs within-person ictal causation. |
| P02, P06, P07, P09 | *Biosynthesis of Catecholamines* (NCBI Basic Neurochemistry) and Dopamine-beta-hydroxylase experimental literature: noradrenaline biosynthesis requires dopamine substrate via dopamine beta-hydroxylase in catecholamine-containing vesicles, with copper, ascorbate, oxygen; upstream synthesis and vesicular proton gradients depend on enzymes and energy. | **Constrains the model:** NE is *synthesised from dopamine* in the relevant neurons; treating dopamine and noradrenaline as exclusive mutually independent brain “fuel modes” is biochemically wrong. However, neural circuits can vary in relative recruitment and state-dependent demand; a specific independently limited cofactor remains a candidate to identify, **not proven**. | Choose candidate resource and expected depletion/accumulation direction from stoichiometry, compartmentation, flux, kinetics and human feasibility *before* interpreting FDS literature. |
| P01, P03, P11 | Systematic review *Ictal autonomic changes as a tool for seizure detection* 2018, PMID 30377843 (epilepsy population). | Physiological autonomic precursors and oscillatory/network state changes are **not unique to FDS**. Epilepsy comparator is necessary to claim syndrome-specific causal signature. | Directly matched event definitions and negative controls. |

## Provisional audit summary (do not conflate layers)
- Supported broadly: diverse functional-seizure recovery symptoms; often measurable autonomic changes.
- Unsettled: within-person predictive thresholds, specificity, physiological relevance of any proposed reserve variable.
- Not established: preferential dopamine-coupled resource depletion causing FDS; universal dopamine-primary/NE-compensatory switching; low dopamine being an FDS adaptation; toxification/clearance as common seizure driver.
- Required: mechanistically interpretable variable and validated CNS proxy, measurable direction and lead/lag, patient-level replication, FDS/epilepsy/panic controls, alternative-model discriminant and no intervention-provocation.

No website publication or medical recommendation is authorised by this audit.
