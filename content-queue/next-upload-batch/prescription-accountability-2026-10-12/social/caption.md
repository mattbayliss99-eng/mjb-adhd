# Personal-account social post

FIVE YEARS OF TREATMENT. ABOUT TEN MONTHS WITHOUT CONTINUITY.

I finally have an Amfexa prescription on its way. ADHD Direct confirmed it was posted on 12 October. I found the private route, assembled the evidence and funded the appointment myself, using my PIP backpay.

The email audit found 4,356 sent-folder messages across my two accounts this year, including wider correspondence and advocacy. There were 305 calendar days between the recorded CWPT discharge and today's prescription dispatch.

During that time I was dealing with severe pain, loss of function and the ADHD symptoms that made all this admin disproportionately difficult. My experience too often felt like monitored decline, referrals and apologies, without an accepted prescribing handover.

Some staff did engage and try to help. I appreciate that. The system still left me doing the coordination needed to secure treatment.

Now my priority is getting healthy. Then accountability and change: what was decided, who owned the next step, and why did repeated escalation fail to secure continuity?

The prescription has been sent; delivery, dispensing and recovery are still ahead. This does not close the accountability questions.

Nobody should have to build their own support system while struggling to function just to reach a treatment decision.
