# Amfexa restart — a few more everyday changes

*Draft continuation to the 14 October entry; do not publish without review.*

During the early period after restarting my prescribed Amfexa (dexamfetamine), I've noticed a difference that's difficult to convey with the usual phrase "better concentration".

Sometimes I can **continue moving while thinking**, instead of having to stop and consciously manage each movement. It feels as though some parts of posture and movement can run more automatically in the background, leaving more attention for whatever I'm trying to do. This is a personal description of function, not a claim that any neurological pathway has been repaired.

There were small practical examples today. I managed to change bedding after a spill; the activity was tiring, but not as incapacitating as a previous similar task had felt. I also managed a second shower in one day and completed toothbrushing while waiting for the water to warm up. That combination of task initiation, task switching and physical activity would previously have felt much less attainable.

I also experience some severe nerve-like discomfort differently in the period surrounding Amfexa treatment, sometimes describing improvement as gradual rather than immediate. This is a subjective impression, not established analgesic efficacy, and I still need the underlying neurological symptoms investigated.

**Important limitations:** These are short-term, uncontrolled, self-reported observations. Sleep, pain, timing, other medicines, supplements, daily variation and expectations were not held constant. They do not establish that Amfexa caused every improvement or that it treats an underlying movement disorder. I will continue to record ordinary functional changes, limitations and adverse effects.

*Personal treatment experience; not prescribing advice. Publication remains subject to author review.*
